# Dataset curation note

This dataset contains two hundred synthetic question and answer examples for an AfyaPlus operational assistant. Its sources are the user supplied domain brief, the Week Four classroom workflow themes, and the authored teaching SOP in policies.json. No patient record, production export, contact detail, credential, or confidential facility document was used. Classroom examples informed topic selection, but their answers and numerical policy promises were not copied into this dataset.

The teaching SOP defines one hundred distinct administrative scenarios across appointments, triage escalation routing, registration, and system access. Each scenario has two question formulations and one reviewed reference workflow. This is deliberate paraphrase augmentation, not two hundred independent policies. Every answer cites a stable source identifier and is checked against the corresponding SOP guidance. The policies are educational assumptions awaiting approval from facility owners; they must not be represented as established AfyaPlus procedures.

Quality criteria require clear ownership, practical next steps, respectful language, limited information sharing, and an explicit boundary around clinical decisions. Answers avoid diagnoses, medicine choices, doses, clinical prioritization, invented deadlines, and unauthorized account access. Emergency scenarios route users to clinical responders without administrative delay. The preparation script adds the mandatory disclaimer, checks required fields, rejects duplicate questions and possible identifiers, verifies source alignment, and writes reproducible validation evidence.

Splitting occurs by scenario rather than individual wording. Both paraphrases remain together, yielding one hundred sixty training examples, twenty validation examples, and twenty test examples. All four domains appear in every split. Consequently, the test set represents ten independent scenarios, a limitation that must accompany reported metrics.

Gaps include approved local policy, multilingual phrasing, accessibility testing, ambiguous requests, evolving workflows, and adversarial conversations. Evaluation supplies the relevant SOP directly, measuring grounded instruction following rather than retrieval accuracy. Human workflow review and unseen scenario testing are required before any operational deployment decision.
