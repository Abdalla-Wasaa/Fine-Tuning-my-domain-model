# Dataset curation note

This replacement corpus contains two hundred source-derived questions and answers for AfyaPlus administrative work in Kenya. Its authoritative sources are the Data Protection Act, the Health Act, and the Data Protection General Regulations, downloaded from Kenya Law. Versioned links, document checksums, physical PDF pages, and clause references accompany the examples. Original documents and their attribution notices are retained for inspection.

One hundred operational interpretations cover appointment access, registration, system access, and administrative escalation. Each interpretation has two question formulations. Answers paraphrase specific source provisions into bounded administrative actions; they are not copied patient records, published facility procedures, or evidence that AfyaPlus has adopted a particular workflow. AI assisted the transformations. Exact citation anchors are checked automatically against the corresponding document section and page, but this check does not establish legal correctness or clinical appropriateness.

Wasaa Abdalla confirmed reviewing and approving all cases. Approval was recorded on September 15, 2026, following his explicit instruction, covering both questions, answers, cited provisions, exceptions, and current applicability. Training refuses unsigned or changed examples. Review hashes bind approval to the exact wording and citation. No clinician endorsement or instructor exception is inferred from a passing structural test.

Quality criteria require understandable administrative ownership, accurate source attribution, limited disclosure, and no diagnosis, prescribing, clinical prioritization, or binding legal conclusions. The mandatory non-diagnostic disclaimer appears in every formatted answer. Uncertain decisions go to qualified healthcare staff or the responsible data team. Specific deadlines appear only where cited provisions support them.

Scenario groups remain disjoint across deterministic training, validation, and test splits of one hundred sixty, twenty, and twenty records. Tokenizer checks reject excessive lengths before training. Gaps include facility-specific booking procedures, source amendments, multilingual requests, accessibility, and retrieval accuracy. The supplied-context evaluation measures instruction following across ten independent test scenarios, not patient outcomes or general clinical competence.
